---

## The ones that get you banned

1. **No griefing.** Do not break, burn or flood anything you did not build.
2. **No stealing.** Chests belong to whoever put them there, even unlocked.
3. **No cheating.** No x-ray, no fly, no automation that plays for you.
4. **No hate, harassment or slurs.** In chat, in signs, in builds, anywhere.
5. **Nothing that ruins the server for everyone.** Lag machines, spam,
   thousands of hoppers in one chunk.

## The ones that get you a word first

- Keep builds a reasonable distance apart. Nobody wants a neighbour on top of
  them.
- Clean up after yourself: fill in your holes, put out your fires.
- Keep chat friendly. Swearing is fine, aiming it at somebody is not.
- Take arguments to a private channel, not into public chat.

## Redstone and farms

Farms are welcome. Farms that tick a thousand entities are not. If the server
starts to lag and the trail leads to your build, we will ask you to turn it
down before we turn it off.

## Appeals

Bans are not forever unless they are. Ask in the Discord, explain what
happened, and somebody will take a look.
